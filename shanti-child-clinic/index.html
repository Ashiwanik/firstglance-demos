Breathing counter

Count the breaths for one minute.

Fast breathing is the earliest sign of pneumonia in a small child. Lift the shirt, watch the tummy rise, and tap once for every breath. The child should be calm or asleep, not crying.

Loose motions

How much ORS, and how often.

Most diarrhoea is treated at home with ORS and zinc. The danger is water loss, so the job is to replace what each loose stool takes out.

ORS after each loose stool
Zinc, once a day
How to give it

Making and giving ORS

  • Mix one full packet in exactly 1 litre of clean drinking water. Use it within 24 hours.
  • Give small sips from a spoon or cup. If the child vomits, wait 10 minutes and start again, slower.
  • Keep breastfeeding and keep giving normal food: khichdi, dal-rice, curd, banana.
  • No cola, packaged juice or glucose water in place of ORS. They can make stools worse.
  • Come at once for blood in the stool, repeated vomiting, sunken eyes, no urine, or a child too sleepy to drink.

Amounts and zinc doses follow WHO/UNICEF home treatment guidance.

Chamki bukhar: three things every night.

Acute Encephalitis Syndrome strikes children here in the hot months, often before dawn. Bihar's health department teaches three steps. They take five minutes and they save lives.

  1. खिलाओFeed

    Never let a child sleep on an empty stomach. Give a full meal at night, with something sweet if they will take it.

  2. जगाओWake

    Wake the child early in the morning and check. Are they alert? Any fever, jerking or unusual sleepiness?

  3. अस्पताल ले जाओGo to hospital

    High fever, fits, a clenched jaw or fainting: come straight away, at any hour. Do not wait for daylight.

Keep children out of the afternoon sun, give plenty of water and ORS, and don't let them eat unripe or fallen litchis on an empty stomach.

Questions

Before you come

Is the clinic really open at night?

Yes. The clinic is listed as open 24 hours. If you are travelling from far at night, call first so the staff expect you.

What should I bring?

The child's vaccination card, any old prescriptions and reports, the medicines you have already given, and a note of the temperature readings with times.

Should I give paracetamol before coming?

If your doctor has already told you the dose for your child's weight, you can give it. Do not give aspirin or adult tablets to a child. A baby under 3 months with fever should be seen before any medicine.

My child missed a vaccine. Is it too late?

Almost never. Bring the card and the doctor will plan the catch-up doses.

Do you see adults too?

Yes. Dr. Rajesh Kumar is a physician as well as a child specialist.
