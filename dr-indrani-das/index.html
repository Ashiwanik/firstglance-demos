Meet Dr Indrani

Dr Indrani Das is a consultant dermatologist with an MBBS and an MD in Dermatology from Gauhati Medical College. She sees patients for acne, pigmentation, hair fall, dandruff and skin allergies.

"I'm here to help you make informed choices about your skin."

Her approach: science-backed advice, care that's personal to you, and no shortcuts.
