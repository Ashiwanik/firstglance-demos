How it works

Five steps from your roof to someone else's raw material.

  1. Tell us the load

    Use the estimator or send a photo. You get a price range or a per-panel quote before anything moves.

  2. We collect, or you drop off

    Scheduled pickup, a bin left on site, or drive in and unload. Your choice per job.

  3. Weigh and docket

    Everything crosses the scale in front of you. The docket shows weight, grade and rate.

  4. Take it apart properly

    Frames, junction boxes and cable come off first. Glass, silicon and metals are separated mechanically.

  5. Paid and certified

    Payment for the metal, and a recovery certificate for your records that shows where each material went.

Compliance without the homework

The tip can't take your panels. We can.

Old panels are a legal responsibility as well as a waste problem. Sunsmith's paperwork is designed to answer the questions an auditor, insurer or council will ask.

Landfill is off the table in Victoria

Solar panels are classed as e-waste, and Victoria has banned e-waste from landfill since 1 July 2019.

The duty sits with your business

Under Victoria's General Environmental Duty, in force since 1 July 2021, a business must take reasonable steps to make sure its waste goes to a lawful place.

So we record the whole trip

Every load gets a chain-of-custody record from your site to our scale to the buyer of each recovered material.

Straight answers

Asked at the gate every day.

Do you pay for solar panels?

Usually not. Panels are mostly glass, so they carry a small recycling fee per panel. The aluminium frames, cable and racking that come with them do have value, and we credit that against the fee.

How do I get the best price for scrap?

Separate it. Copper away from brass, clean aluminium away from painted, cable in its own pile. Sorted loads grade higher and weigh in faster.

What's the smallest load you'll take?

One panel or one bucket of fittings at drop-off. Pickups make sense from about a ute load up. Ask and we'll tell you which is cheaper for you.

Can you take the whole system off the roof?

Yes. Panels, racking, inverter and cable can leave on one truck with one certificate.
